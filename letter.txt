🌿 Poem: The Road of Tomorrow (Class 10 style)

The road of tomorrow lies quiet and wide,
With dreams like birds flying close by our side.
We walk with hope, though shadows may fall,
For courage grows stronger when fear grows tall.

The morning sun whispers, “Rise once again,”
Wipe off the worries, the sorrow, the pain.
Each step is a lesson, each turn is a guide,
Failures may follow—but so does pride.

Like rivers that wander and still find the sea,
Our hearts hold a promise of what we can be.
So trust in your journey, keep lighting your way—
Tomorrow is built by the choices of today.
